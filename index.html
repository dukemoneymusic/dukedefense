HOW TO PLAY

1 · The Apple

A golden apple sits at the end of every route. Vermin walk the street to reach it, and every one that arrives takes a bite. Lose all the bites and the district falls.

2 · Emplace Anywhere

Pick a weapon off the tray, then set it down anywhere that isn't road or building. A green footprint means clear ground; red means find somewhere else.

3 · Your Sidearm

With no weapon selected, click to shoot. It won't carry a wave on its own, but it finishes wounded runners and pays 25% extra bounty.

4 · Shoot the Drops

Kills and supply drops leave cash, ammo crates and medkits lying in the street. Shoot them to collect. They expire, so keep scanning.

5 · Armour

Ball ammo gets punished hard by plate. Bring HE, incendiary or energy against gators, golems, and anything with a caution sign for a head.

6 · Move the Camera

The battlefield is far bigger than the window. Right-drag or WASD to pan, wheel to zoom, and click the minimap to jump across the map.
